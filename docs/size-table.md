### After phase 1 step 1.5: the ocean

[Step 1.5](phase1-plan.md#15-geometry-renderer)'s first renders showed that most open ocean was missing. The ocean reader (`measure/water.rs`, used by `measure` and `build`) assumed the shapefile's ring winding, which `water-polygons-split` does not follow, and dropped every ocean piece without islands. **Every water size above undercounts the ocean** for that reason. Rebuilt with the fix (North America, MiB):

| | 1.3 pack | 1.5 pack | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Water tiles | 3,674.5 | 4,710.7 | +1,036.2 |
| All tiles | 7,886.7 | 8,922.9 | +1,036.2 |
| Tile index (`TIDX`) | 112 | 275 | +163 |
| Tiles stored | 23.7M | 64.0M | +40.3M |
| Pack | 8,001 | 9,200 | +1,199 |

Almost all of it is open ocean inside the region's `.poly`: tiles at z13–z14 that hold nothing but one ocean square. Quebec's pack grew from 742.5 to 751.0 MiB; Ohio has no ocean. The North America total becomes **about 12,950 MiB (12.6 GiB, 13.6 GB)**. Leaving out full-ocean tiles whose parent is also full ocean (the app would draw the parent) would win most of it back; that is [phase 1 step 1.5b](phase1-plan.md#15b-leave-out-full-ocean-tiles).

## What the columns mean

- **Ohio, Ontario, Quebec** are measured on their own Geofabrik extracts. Ontario and Quebec clip the ocean to their `.poly`.
- **North America** is Geofabrik's `north-america` extract, measured in 3 bands. Tiles, routing and search include Mexico and Greenland. Addresses cover the US and Canada.
- **Planet** scales every North America row by the planet PBF's size over North America's: 95.5 GB / 19.5 GB = **4.90×** (`planet-261019`). This is crude:
  - CoMaps' own planet-to-North-America ratio is **4.27×**, which would put the planet at about **50 GiB**. The two give a range of 50–57 GiB without driveways, 50–58 GiB with them.
  - **Addresses do not scale like that.** North America has unusually good open address data (OpenAddresses, NAR, TIGER). Elsewhere coverage ranges from complete (much of Europe) to almost none, so the 1.8 GiB shown is only a placeholder.
  - Either way, the planet is well under roadmap §4's 100 GB stretch target.
- **CoMaps** sums the `.mwm` files of CoMaps' `countries.txt` (version 261004, map series 2026.06.28) that cover the same ground:
  - Ohio: 4 files.
  - Ontario: 12. Quebec: 9.
  - North America: the US (minus Guam, Puerto Rico and the Minor Outlying Islands, which are not in Geofabrik's extract), Canada, Mexico and Greenland, 216 files.
  - Planet: all 1,155 files, including `World` and `WorldCoasts`.

## Commentary

**North America is 11.8 GiB (12.6 GB) with driveways, 11.6 GiB (12.5 GB) as measured without them.** That is under the 15 GB stop line, but still over roadmap §4's 6–10 GB target. It fits on a 16 GB USB drive.

**Where the bytes are** (North America):

| Part | MiB | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Water tiles | 3,724 | 31% |
| CH (estimated) | 3,276 | 28% |
| Land cover tiles | 2,053 | 17% |
| Road tiles | 1,926 | 16% |
| Addresses (A3 + TIGER) | 365 | 3% |
| Search | 253 | 2% |
| Other tiles and the tile index | 307 | 3% |

**Against CoMaps:** about half the size, region by region (0.51–0.70). CoMaps includes buildings, paths, walking and cycling routing, and more POIs, all of which this pack leaves out. It does not include most of the OpenAddresses, NAR and TIGER addresses that this pack has. Quebec is the closest to CoMaps, probably because its dense CanVec water is in both packs.

**How phase 0 got here** (North America total):

| Step | Total (MiB) | Change |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0.5: first full run | 13,350 | |
| Water simplified (decision 14) | 12,335 | −1,015 |
| Land cover simplified (decision 15) | about 11,940 | −396 |
| Driveways out of tiles (decision 16) | about 11,800 | −144 |
| Tile index counted (it was left out of the totals before) | 11,905 | +109 |
| Driveways put back (decision 16, revised) | **about 12,049** | +144 |

**The rest of the gap to 10 GB**, should it matter later:
- **The CH is an estimate** at 12 bytes per node and per edge. Phase 3 builds its own and may do better (e.g. edge IDs relative to the node, or a smaller weight).
- **Water** is still the largest layer. Dropping the smallest ponds at z14, or streams below z14, would cut it further. Both lose features, which decision 14 avoided.
- **V5** (4×4 tile groups) is another 8% smaller on tiles, at 2.4–2.6× the bytes read per viewport.
- Size is not urgent: the owner considers about 12 GiB workable (decision 16).

**Licensing:** the address rows include OpenAddresses sources that name no license (decision 13). Without them North America has 86.8M addresses instead of 151.5M.

## Reproduce

With `B=target/release-builder/omp-builder`, `I=$OMP_WORK/inputs`, `A=$OMP_WORK/measure/na` (the 0.5 address runs), `M=$OMP_WORK/measure/final`, `NA=$I/osm/north-america-261001.osm.pbf` and `cap` meaning `systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=56G -p MemorySwapMax=0`:

```
$B fetch --region north-america --sources osm,poly,openaddresses,tiger,nar,water
$B measure $I/osm/ohio-261001.osm.pbf    --json $M/ohio.json
$B measure $I/osm/ontario-261001.osm.pbf --json $M/ontario.json --clip-poly $I/poly/ontario.poly
$B measure $I/osm/quebec-261001.osm.pbf  --json $M/quebec.json  --clip-poly $I/poly/quebec.poly
for k in 0 1 2; do   # band 0 also counts the routing graph
  cap $B measure $NA --region north-america --band $k/3 --routing $([ $k = 0 ] && echo graph || echo none) \
    --clip-poly $I/poly/north-america.poly --json $M/north-america-$k.json
done
cap $B addr-spike $NA --subdivision all --each --sources osm,openaddresses,nar,tiger --json $A/addr
$B report --sum "North America" --sum-json $A/addr/sum-na.json $A/addr/US-*.json $A/addr/CA-*.json
$B report $M/ohio.json $M/ontario.json $M/quebec.json $M/north-america-*.json > $M/report.md
curl -o $M/comaps/countries-261004.txt https://codeberg.org/comaps/comaps/raw/branch/main/data/countries.txt
$B report --size-table $M/ohio.json $M/ontario.json $M/quebec.json $M/north-america-*.json \
  --addr ohio=$A/addr/US-OH.json --addr ontario=$A/addr/CA-ON.json --addr quebec=$A/addr/CA-QC.json \
  --addr north-america=$A/addr/sum-na.json --planet north-america=95507771401 \
  --comaps $M/comaps/countries-261004.txt --comaps-ids ohio=Ohio --comaps-ids ontario=Canada_Ontario \
  --comaps-ids quebec=Canada_Quebec --comaps-ids planet=Countries \
  --comaps-ids "north-america=United States of America,-US_Guam,-US_Puerto Rico,-US_United States Minor Outlying Islands,Canada,Mexico,Greenland"
```

- `95507771401` is the size of `planet-261019.osm.pbf` in bytes.
- CoMaps' `countries.txt` changes with each map release; the copy used here is kept in `$M/comaps/`.
- The full per-variant, per-layer and per-zoom tables are in `$M/report.md`. The address tables are in `$A/addr-table-0.5.md`.
